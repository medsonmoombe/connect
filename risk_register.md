# Risk Register

This document outlines potential risks to the Energy Capital Match project, along with mitigation strategies.

| ID | Risk Category      | Description                                                                 | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy                                                                                             |
|----|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|--------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | Technology         | Gemini API performance or accuracy does not meet expectations.                | Medium     | High   | - Implement robust error handling and retry logic. <br>- Develop a fallback to a purely rule-based score. <br>- Continuously monitor and fine-tune AI prompts. |
| 2  | Market Adoption    | Difficulty attracting a critical mass of projects and investors in Zambia.    | Medium     | High   | - Engage in targeted business development and marketing pre-launch. <br>- Partner with local industry associations. <br>- Offer initial incentives for early adopters. |
| 3  | Execution          | Delays in development phases push back the launch date.                       | High       | Medium | - Adopt agile development practices. <br>- Prioritize MVP features ruthlessly. <br>- Maintain clear and constant communication with stakeholders. |
| 4  | Regulatory         | Changes in Zambian energy or financial regulations impact the platform's model. | Low        | High   | - Retain local legal counsel to stay informed of the regulatory landscape. <br>- Design the platform to be flexible to accommodate policy changes. |
| 5  | Data Security      | A data breach exposes sensitive project or user information.                  | Low        | High   | - Adhere strictly to security best practices (RLS, signed URLs, etc.). <br>- Conduct a third-party security audit before launch. <br>- Implement a clear data privacy policy. |
